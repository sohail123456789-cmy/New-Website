About DigitalDot

Small details. Bigger possibilities.

We build websites and web applications for US businesses — scoped around what each business actually needs, not a one-size-fits-all package.

What we believe

A website should be an asset, not a liability. That means clean, maintainable code; a scope that matches the business, not an oversized project; and a site built for search and speed from day one, not fixed after launch.

Who we work with

We work with US businesses of every size — startups shipping a first website, small and medium-sized businesses modernizing an outdated one, and enterprises with more complex technical requirements.

A note on credentials

We don't publish invented years-of-experience claims, fabricated awards, or unverified client logos. As we complete real projects and gather genuine feedback, this page — and our portfolio — will reflect that honestly.
