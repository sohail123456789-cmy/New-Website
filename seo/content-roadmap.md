# 90-Day Content & SEO Roadmap

Priorities below follow the execution-rules ordering (technical readiness → core landing pages → high-intent keywords → portfolio → supporting content → performance-informed iteration → legitimate outreach).

## Days 1–14: Launch readiness
- Verify which services (ecommerce, custom apps, WordPress, Shopify, maintenance) are genuinely deliverable; remove or revise any page that overstates capability.
- Choose and purchase the production domain; replace the `https://www.digitaldot.com/` placeholder across every canonical tag, OG tag, JSON-LD block, `sitemap.xml`, and `robots.txt`.
- Set up GA4 and Search Console per `analytics-audit.md`; submit the sitemap.
- Collect at least 1–2 real, permissioned portfolio examples to replace the illustrative placeholders on `/portfolio.html`.

## Days 15–45: Core landing pages (already built, now to refine with real data)
- Once Search Console has 2–4 weeks of data, compare actual impressions/clicks against the predicted priorities in `keyword-opportunities.md` and reprioritize if needed.
- Expand the two highest-priority service pages (`/services/website-development.html`, `/services/wordpress-development.html` — lowest SD in the research) with any additional detail or FAQ content that real client questions surface.

## Days 46–70: Portfolio and case studies
- Publish 2–3 genuine case studies with real outcomes (only after client permission). Do not publish numeric results that cannot be verified.
- Add a dedicated case-study page per project if there's enough real content to support one; otherwise keep them as portfolio entries.

## Days 71–90: Supporting content
Potential article topics (write only once there's a genuine point of view or data to share — do not mass-produce thin content):
- "How much does a business website cost?" (ties to `website development company` / `website development company us` cluster)
- "How to choose a web development company" (ties to the broader `website development company` cluster)
- "Website redesign: how to plan it without losing SEO value" (ties to `website redesign services` cluster, SD 35, 6,600 vol — the second-highest volume term found)
- "Ecommerce platform comparison for small businesses" (ties to the `ecommerce website development` cluster, which also carries the highest CPC found, $96.93)
- "What's included in ongoing website maintenance?" (ties to `website maintenance services`, SD 21)

## Ongoing
- Legitimate outreach and authority-building (guest contributions, genuine partnerships) only — no link schemes or fabricated citations.
- Re-run Ubersuggest research quarterly to catch new opportunities and confirm existing difficulty/volume figures haven't shifted materially.
